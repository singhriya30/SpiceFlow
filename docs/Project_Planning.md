**Project Name:** SpiceFlow
**Project Type:** Business Management & Customer Engagement Platform
**Business:** Organic Masala Brand

# 1. Project Vision

SpiceFlow is a complete business operating system designed for an organic spice company. Rather than being just an online shopping website, it aims to manage every stage of the business—from inventory and customer orders to marketing campaigns, customer engagement, analytics, automation, and AI-powered features.

The long-term vision is to create software that not only simplifies business operations but also builds a memorable customer experience. Customers should enjoy interacting with the brand through recipe content, QR-code experiences, collectible cards, weekly challenges, reward systems, and social media activities. At the same time, the software should reduce manual work for the business owner by automating repetitive tasks such as order processing, inventory tracking, customer communication, reporting, and marketing workflows.

The platform should be scalable enough to support future products, additional employees, multiple warehouses, AI assistants, automation workflows, and advanced analytics without requiring a complete redesign.



# 2. Business Goal

The primary goal of this software is to provide a centralized platform that manages every important aspect of the organic spice business.

Currently, many small businesses use different tools for inventory, customer records, payments, spreadsheets, marketing, and order tracking. This results in duplicated work, inconsistent data, and wasted time.

SpiceFlow will solve this problem by bringing everything into one system.

The software will allow the business owner to:

* Manage organic spice products from a single dashboard.
* Monitor inventory in real time.
* Receive and process customer orders efficiently.
* Track payments and revenue.
* Coordinate employees and delivery personnel.
* Launch marketing campaigns.
* Increase customer retention through interactive experiences.
* Analyze customer behavior and business performance.
* Automate repetitive operational tasks.
* Prepare the business for future AI-powered capabilities.

Instead of being just an e-commerce website, SpiceFlow will become the company's digital operating system.



# 3. Business Objectives

The software should help achieve the following business objectives:

### Operational Efficiency

Reduce manual work by automating order processing, inventory updates, invoice generation, customer notifications, and reporting.

### Business Growth

Support increasing numbers of products, customers, employees, and daily orders without changing the overall system architecture.

# 5. Business Workflow

The complete business workflow is expected to operate as follows:

1. The owner adds organic spice products along with prices, descriptions, images, stock quantities, and categories.

2. Customers visit the website, browse available products, and add desired items to their shopping cart.

3. Customers place orders and complete payment through the supported payment gateway.

4. The system automatically records the order and notifies the owner or assigned employee.

5. Inventory is verified before order confirmation.

6. Employees prepare and pack the products.

7. Every package includes a campaign insert such as:

* QR Challenge Card
* Recipe Card
* Lucky Draw Card
* Secret Mission Card
* Golden Spoon Campaign
* Collectible Spice Card

8. The order is assigned to a delivery partner.

9. Customers receive real-time order updates.

10. Delivery is completed.

11. Customers can rate products and delivery experience.

12. Customers scan the QR code inside the package to unlock interactive content.

13. The owner monitors sales, inventory, campaign participation, customer engagement, and business performance through the dashboard.

# 7. Marketing & Customer Engagement Vision

One of the unique selling points of the business is that every spice package should provide customers with an experience rather than just a product. The software should support marketing campaigns that encourage repeat purchases, customer participation, and organic social media sharing.

### QR Code Experiences

Every package can include a unique QR code that unlocks content such as recipe videos, cooking tips, hidden rewards, educational content about spices, or limited-time promotions.

### Weekly Missions

Customers receive new challenges every week, such as cooking a recipe using the purchased spice, solving a puzzle, identifying spices, or completing simple family activities. Completing missions earns reward points.

### Secret Masala Cards

Each package may contain collectible cards featuring spice facts, recipes, cooking hacks, regional cuisines, or surprise challenges. Limited-edition cards encourage repeat purchases.

### Scratch & Win Rewards

Customers can reveal discount coupons, cashback offers, free products, or bonus reward points after completing purchases or scanning QR codes.

### Lucky Draw Campaigns

Each order automatically enters eligible customers into seasonal lucky draws where they can win kitchen products, gift hampers, or exclusive merchandise.

### Referral Program

Customers receive referral codes that reward both the existing customer and the new customer after successful purchases.

### Recipe Experience

Customers can access professionally created recipe videos, downloadable recipe cards, cooking guides, and nutritional information using products they have purchased.

### User-Generated Content Campaigns

Customers are encouraged to create Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and social media posts by participating in cooking contests, recipe challenges, or creative packaging campaigns. The software should track participation and reward eligible users.

### Loyalty Program

Customers earn reward points for purchases, referrals, reviews, challenge completion, and campaign participation. Points can later be redeemed for discounts or exclusive products.

### Customer Analytics

The owner should be able to measure campaign success by tracking participation rates, QR-code scans, referral conversions, reward redemption, repeat purchases, and customer engagement.

# 1. Introduction

This document defines the functional and non-functional requirements for the SpiceFlow platform. It serves as the official reference for the design, development, testing, and future enhancement of the system.

# 4. Business Rules

The following rules shall always be enforced by the system:

* Stock quantity shall never become negative.
* Every product shall have a unique SKU.
* Every order shall belong to exactly one customer.
* Every invoice shall have a unique invoice number.
* Customers shall not purchase products that are out of stock.
* Only the Owner shall be permitted to create employee or delivery accounts.
* Employees shall not have access to financial reports unless explicitly authorized.
* Delivery personnel shall not modify product, pricing, or inventory information.
* Coupons shall only be redeemable within their validity period.
* Reward points shall never have a negative balance.
* Every QR code generated by the system shall be unique.
* Deleted products shall not appear in the customer storefront but may remain archived for reporting purposes.
* Orders marked as **Delivered** shall become read-only except for returns, refunds, or administrative actions.
* Every inventory update shall be recorded in the inventory history log for auditing purposes.
* Every payment transaction shall be linked to a valid customer order.
